The SVG viewport is normalized to 48 x 48. Stroke width is set to 3.4 to match
the application's folder glyph. Paths are unchanged and use one color.
Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners.

Kugou, Kuwo and Qishui SVGs were supplied by the maintainer from iconfont.
Their fills use the interface color. The Qishui foreground mark is retained
with a cropped viewport; the export's background is omitted.
